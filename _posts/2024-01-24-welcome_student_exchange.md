During the third day of welcoming international exchange students to the University of the Andes, I had the opportunity to represent the student initiative I am part of, the “Tuna Uniandina.” Part of the event was dedicated to talking about Colombian culture, where different initiatives characteristic of the university were presented. It was a real privilege to be able to show a non-Spanish-speaking audience the musical roots and traditional costumes that represent our Colombian plains so well.
