## GymEdge Exercise Prediction
How often have you had to replay a video or ask a coach just to confirm the correct technique for an exercise? We've built a solution to solve exactly that!

Using only an IMU sensor, we successfully recorded and predicted the certainty/accuracy of different aerobics exercises, enabling real-time technique improvement. In the future, this system could be expanded to provide valuable health statistics regarding daily exercise volume and consistency.


## Capacitive Sensor for Bacteriophage Detection
Design of a capacitive IoT sensor capable of the detection of bacteriophages within the subtamces as an alternative for traditional methods.

## Surface PCB welding furnace
During one of the final year courses of my bachelor's degree. I worked in the coding and design of a DIY furnace. We were one of the most outstanding teams within the class by designing a low-cost, small and effective furnace able to control the heat of the chamber and reaching a high controlled temperature of 300 °C.
